About

Professional Biography

I'm an undergraduate at Tulane University pursuing a double major in Real Estate and Political Science, originally from the Potomac, Maryland area. My interest sits where markets, policy, and the built environment meet — understanding not just how a deal pencils, but how zoning, local politics, and demographics shape what gets built and where.

Through Tulane's real estate development curriculum, I've underwritten full development deals from site selection through stabilized NOI, including a 266-unit build-to-rent community in Baldwin County, Alabama. Outside the classroom I'm active in the Tulane Real Estate Group, Wall Street Krewe, and the Tulane Algorithmic Trading Club, and I'm pursuing opportunities in development, investments, and debt & equity in the Southeast and Texas.
